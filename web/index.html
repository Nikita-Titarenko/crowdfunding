Sepolia flow

AuthorRegistry and RewardToken are fixed on Sepolia. This app creates and loads campaigns dynamically.

Grant the loaded campaign permission to mint reward tokens. This requires `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` on RewardToken.

Withdraw campaign funds to the beneficiary after the deadline if the goal was reached.

Claim a refund after the deadline if the campaign did not reach its goal.

Claim CRWD rewards after a successful campaign. The campaign must already have `MINTER_ROLE`.

Connect MetaMask to work with Sepolia contracts.
